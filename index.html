Prototype footage & limitations

This is a full-screen cinematic interaction prototype demonstrating a storyboard and an educational choice engine, not a finished episode. The stock footage shows real people, but does not portray the fictional character “Sky”. The digital set decorations and English fictional dialogue are illustrative, not attached to or spoken by the filmed performers. No original film cast, room reconstruction or synchronized facial reactions have been produced yet.
