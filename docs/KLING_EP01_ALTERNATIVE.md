# GAME FILM — alternative original child-character cinema pipeline

**Reason:** Google Flow sometimes rejects even harmless fictional 11–12-year-old movie characters; that does not mean the GAME FILM concept is prohibited everywhere, or that we should age the characters to adults. Keep the project creative canon: Sky and Emma are fictional 11–12-year-old friends, the story is a family movie about patience and listening.

## Check Kling before spending anything

- Official home: https://kling.ai/
- Community policy: https://kling.ai/docs/community-policy
- Free credits: Kling says daily free credits are available. Free video outputs may bear a visible watermark. **Verify actual balance, model eligibility, and current commercial/public distribution terms in the account before rendering.**
- The policy explicitly forbids depicting abuse, exploitation, sexualization or endangerment of minors. We require only original, age-appropriate, benign content.
- Kling's reference features may help keep character faces, costumes and the room coherent across edited clips, but availability may depend on plan.
- Never substitute adults for the canon child characters without creative approval. No real child images or personal data; no third-party or influencer likeness.

## First test (child-friendly, with no clothing-change action)

**Goal:** test that a provider can generate two original fictional young characters naturally acting in a simple family-friendly scene. One small clip before committing to production. Do not attempt to bypass a model's moderation if the provider rejects it.

```text
Original family movie scene with two fictional girl friends, Sky (11) and Emma (12), entirely invented and not modeled on any real child. Both are fully clothed in ordinary everyday clothes. Sky has a dark-brown wavy bob, freckles and a lilac cardigan. Emma has a short auburn bob, green-brown eyes, a teal shirt and a peach vest.

In a bright, cozy family living room with a soft lilac wall, oak floor, oval mirror and warm afternoon daylight, Emma comes through the doorway, notices Sky waiting, and walks toward her. They smile at one another and share a friendly wave. Natural human acting and hands; authentic children's family film, realistic lighting, high quality camera tracking from medium-wide to a natural two-shot, real depth of field, 9:16 portrait. No costume change or dressing action in this clip. Nonsexual, nonviolent, age-appropriate. No existing people, names or brands. No captions.
```

Only if this harmless test is accepted, assess: natural movement, photorealism, actor stability and permitted use. Next scene can use persistent character references, but respect provider safety and specific feature restrictions.

## How other film creators achieve consistency

1. Lock character identity by creating original character-reference images.
2. Generate short 5–10 second shots, using the same references, opening/ending frames and consistent wardrobe.
3. Add natural voices, camera cuts, and edited sound, then stitch the clips locally.
4. In GAME FILM, the next accepted shot changes only after a user's authored interactive choice. Do not do API-generated video per child.

## Production status

Google Flow rejected our first photorealistic scene. This does **not** prove all fictional minor content is disallowed. Google specifically flags some generations and disallows **editing uploaded videos of identifiable minors**; restrictions on generative clips can be conservative. The adult-only Flow test is an optional separate benchmark, **not a replacement for the children's show**.

Neither provider's actual cinematic episode video exists in this repo. Do not publish synthetic test patterns or the 3D avatar sample as though it were realistic filmed content. No added budget or new server.
